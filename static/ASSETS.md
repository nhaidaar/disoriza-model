- `app-home.webp`: existing Disoriza Google Play promotional screenshot, converted to WebP without changing its content. Source: https://play-lh.googleusercontent.com/Y_EkXw2tfsjy-EmcrBbwyupi8dmIf_FxmcOMTteDfOf8wpgHrvfQ6x9dzK-cTrF397Er=w1080
- `app-result.webp`: existing Disoriza Google Play promotional screenshot, converted to WebP without changing its content. Source: https://play-lh.googleusercontent.com/3UNHHz3uJUfJMxjDvzuGpU9VCHnbSuziNazLP9bkr_J_MfT525dnTNgQtSjlKnlVZlBQ=w1080
- The page crops the promotional headings outside each phone viewport using CSS. Screenshot content is from the published app listing, not a live analysis result.
- `fonts/`: DM Sans (regular and semibold) and DM Serif Display (italic), downloaded from Google Fonts. SIL Open Font License files are included alongside the fonts.

- `google-play.svg`: multicolor Google Play prism SVG from the public Google Play listing header; paths and colors preserved. Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.disoriza.app

No generated imagery is used.
